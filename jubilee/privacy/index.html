Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Jubilee does not collect any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Your entries

Names, dates, categories and background photos are stored only on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app and its widgets. Jubilee has no account and no server, and sends nothing over the internet. Your entries are included in your device's backups (for example iCloud Backup) like the data of any other app; those backups are handled by Apple.

Contacts

When you add a person from your contacts, Jubilee reads that contact's name and date. If you allow access to your contacts, Jubilee looks up the phone number and email address of the people you added, so you can message or email them from a reminder. Nothing from your contacts leaves your device.

Photos

A background photo you choose is copied into Jubilee's storage on your device. Jubilee asks for access to your photo library only to bring over the photos you chose in an earlier version of the app.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications that your device schedules itself. No notification service is involved.

No tracking

Jubilee contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party code, and does not track you.

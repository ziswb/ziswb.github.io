Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

Jubilee does not collect any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device – or, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your private iCloud, where only you can access it.

Your entries

Names, dates, categories and background photos are stored on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app and its widgets. Jubilee has no account and no server of its own. Without iCloud sync, it sends nothing over the internet. Your entries are included in your device's backups (for example iCloud Backup) like the data of any other app; those backups are handled by Apple.

iCloud sync (Jubilee Plus)

If you buy Jubilee Plus and turn on iCloud sync, your entries and their background photos are also stored in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), so that your devices signed in to the same Apple Account show the same entries. Only you can access this data; the developer can't see it. It is stored and protected by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

You can turn sync off in Jubilee's settings at any time; your entries then stay on your device. To remove your data from iCloud, tap "Delete Data from iCloud" in Jubilee's settings, or delete Jubilee's data in the Settings app under your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Contacts

When you add a person from your contacts, Jubilee reads that contact's name and date. If you allow access to your contacts, Jubilee looks up the phone number and email address of the people you added, so you can message or email them from a reminder. Phone numbers and email addresses are only looked up, never stored. Jubilee keeps a reference to the contact with the entry; with iCloud sync, this reference goes to your private iCloud along with the entry. Nothing else from your contacts leaves your device.

Photos

A background photo you choose is copied into Jubilee's storage on your device (and, with iCloud sync, into your private iCloud). Jubilee asks for access to your photo library only to bring over the photos you chose in an earlier version of the app.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications that your device schedules itself. No notification service is involved.

Purchases

Jubilee Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Jubilee only learns from Apple whether Jubilee Plus is active for your Apple Account and receives no personal or payment data.

No tracking

Jubilee contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party code, and does not track you.
